# Lin (2025) — TASI lectures on Matrix Theory from a modern viewpoint

**File:** `papers/lin_2025.pdf`

## Full citation

Henry W. Lin, *TASI lectures on Matrix Theory from a modern viewpoint*, arXiv:2508.20970v3 [hep-th] (v3: 18 Aug 2026).

## Main question

Pedagogical review: (Part I) the D0-brane/BFSS matrix quantum mechanics and its gravity dual from a post-AdS/CFT perspective; (Part II, §6) the matrix bootstrap method and its application to BFSS. Only §6 (and §3.1 on finite-$N$ spectra) is directly relevant here.

## Physical system

BFSS matrix QM (9 bosonic + 16 fermionic matrices); single-matrix integrals; bosonic Yang–Mills matrix QM (140); toy supermembrane $V=x^2y^2$.

## Important definitions (§6)

- **Matrix-integral bootstrap**: moments $\langle\mathrm{tr}X^k\rangle$ (92); loop/Schwinger–Dyson equations $\langle\mathrm{tr}X^kV'(X)\rangle=\sum_{\ell}\langle\mathrm{tr}X^\ell\rangle\langle\mathrm{tr}X^{k-\ell-1}\rangle$ (94) with factorisation; Hankel positivity $M_{ij}=\langle\mathrm{tr}X^{i+j}\rangle\succeq0$ (95)–(96).
- **Nonlinear relaxation** (98)–(99): $q\ge x^2$ via $\begin{pmatrix}1&x\\x&q\end{pmatrix}\succeq0$; refined $M\succeq Q$ from covariance positivity.
- **Quantum-mechanical bootstrap** (§6.2): $\langle[H,O]\rangle=0$ (100) in an eigenstate or any stationary density matrix; positivity $\langle O_i^\dagger O_j\rangle\succeq0$ relies only on Hilbert-space positivity — "fermionic degrees of freedom are not a problem". Scanning $E$ produces an "archipelago" of islands for low eigenstates. For large-$N$ matrix QM use single-trace EOM and adjoint positivity; cyclicity + CCR (101) is the only place where $N=\infty$ enters.
- **Finite temperature** (§6.3): energy–entropy balance inequality $\langle O^\dagger O\rangle\log\frac{\langle O^\dagger O\rangle}{\langle OO^\dagger\rangle}\le\beta\langle O^\dagger[H,O]\rangle$ (102); its $\beta\to\infty$ limit is ground-state positivity (103)–(104).
- **Time-dependent bootstrap** (Further reading 5): primal/dual SDP for Lorentzian one-point functions (106)–(113).
- **SUSY ground-state bootstrap** (box after (127)): impose $\langle\{Q_\alpha,O_\alpha\}\rangle=0$ (128); ground-state positivity is then implied [118].
- Level assignment (139): $\ell(X)=1,\ell(P)=2,\ell(\psi)=3/2$.

## Main assumptions

Standard: factorisation at infinite $N$; ground state a singlet with all global symmetries.

## Main analytical results (§6.4)

- Polchinski's virial bound rederived as a bootstrap (114)–(123): $N\langle\mathrm{Tr}X^4\rangle^{1/2}\big(\frac{144}{g^2}\langle\mathrm{Tr}X^4\rangle+\frac{2E}3\big)\ge\frac94g^2N^4$ (123); improved with fermions to $\frac1{N\lambda^{2/3}}\langle\mathrm{Tr}X^2\rangle\ge\frac{3}{16}3^{1/3}\approx0.27$ (126) and the level-6 value $0.2936$ (127) vs Monte Carlo 0.378.
- $SO(9)$ block decomposition of the 4-fermion correlator (129)–(138): $0\le\frac19\langle\mathrm{tr}O^IO^I\rangle\le\frac{64}3$ (138), $1\le\langle\mathrm{tr}OO\rangle\le2$.
- Bosonic $D=2,9$ models: islands at level 10 [104] (Fig. 6) — evidence that BFSS peninsulas (Fig. 5) may close at level $\gtrsim10$.

## Limitations

Review; no new bounds. Bootstrap yields inequalities only; convergence unproven in general; cost $\sim D^L$ in the number of letters $D$ and length $L$.

## Relationship to our project

The cleanest concise statement of the *method* we will use, including the three key facts for us: (i) positivity needs only Hilbert-space positivity, so fermionic models are fine; (ii) at finite $N$ (our case, since the Hilbert space is $2^{pN^2}$-dimensional) one may use $\langle OH\rangle=E\langle O\rangle$ and scan $E$ to resolve individual levels — no factorisation needed; (iii) for BPS states the supercharge constraint (128) is the strongest and simplest formulation. The energy–entropy inequality (102) is the tool if we ever want thermal (finite-$T$) information, e.g. a bootstrap version of the Hagedorn/thermal analyses of Gaitan et al. and Anninos–Silva.
